## Overall judgment

Yes, a referee would likely read parts of v2 as AI-written even without the disclosure, especially the abstract’s self-description, the recurring provenance labels and the process sections. The coordinate calculations and the three-box example read like ordinary mathematical exposition. The main signal is the frequency with which the manuscript previews, qualifies and summarizes its own work. The conspicuous stock words in the brief, such as “delve,” “leverage” and “crucial,” and the literal “not just X but Y” formula are absent. The recurring labels are long, and many are italicized.

## Five changes with the most impact

1. Reshape the abstract around the bound, the coordinate method, the one-box identity and the exact constant. Keep detailed provenance in the notice and main text.
2. Use one short supplementary marker at each relevant passage, with the full definition and check record in one place. Keep Lean identifiers where readers use them.
3. Cut repeated roadmaps and let the equations, the running example and the paragraph giving the next deduction carry the reader through the proof.
4. Replace vague transitions and corrective flourishes with the fact they point to, especially the signed within-column count, the shorter-column estimate and the `IsSmall` ceiling.
5. Condense §§8–10 to the generation method, specific checks and limits of the test. Put model assignments and detailed revision history in the process record.
