**Referee impression.** Yes. Even without the disclosure, the repeated previews, near-duplicate explanations and production-log cadence would make the paper read as machine-written. The mathematical prose is mostly sober. The named vocabulary examples and the stock “not just X but Y” construction are absent. The concrete slot examples and the explanation of the squared norm sound like exposition written for someone following the argument.

**Five changes with the greatest effect**

1. Put the one-box identity and its consequence in a single opening paragraph, followed by the theorem. Move the automorphic setup after the statement.
2. Remove the roadmap and the seven-item proof preview. Keep each failed bound or counterexample beside the step it explains.
3. Turn the six bold literature lead-ins into connected prose. Place the highest-weight argument in one location and point to it briefly elsewhere.
4. Use “AI model checked this outside Lean” wherever the checking status is first needed. Remove “checked by hand” throughout.
5. Keep the abstract on the bound and its coordinate proof. Put survey and grading details in their own sections, and report the method and counts in plain language.
