7. **OK — Theorem B(b–c), including the denominator, orientation and equality (lines 395–412, 925–949).** Fix one ordered choice of source $c$ and target $c'$ in an unordered pair, with heights $a,b$. For $p$ in the source, the signed average on the target plus $p$ is an orthogonal projection $A_p$. The cosets of the target permutation group have representatives $1,(p\,q)$. Acting on an already target-alternating $f$ gives $(b+1)A_pf=(I-Y_p)f$. Thus

   $$
   \frac{\|(I-Y_p)f\|^2}{b+1}=(b+1)\|A_pf\|^2
   =\operatorname{Re}\langle f,(I-Y_p)f\rangle .
   $$

   Summing over $p$ gives $a\|f\|^2-\operatorname{Re}\langle f,X_{cc'}f\rangle$. Choose $a=\min(a,b)$. In Proposition 2 both orders give the *same* $X_{cc'}$, so twice this deficit occurs for each unordered pair. The internal swaps give exactly $-\sum_c h_c(h_c-1)\|f\|^2$. Subtract from B(a) times the squared norm to obtain B(b) exactly. Either orientation in a tie gives the same total loss. All weights are positive, so vanishing is equivalent to every displayed defect vanishing. This also gives the right answer for $f=0$, no pairs, and $n=0$ (when the label map forces $Q=\varnothing$). The one-box identity occurs inside the Lean proof at lines 65–81 of `ReSwapSumLe`. The global sum and its equality criterion are supplementary. In (b–c), the target column in the pair is part of the definition of each $Y_p$. Writing $Y_{p,c'}$ with a primed column index would make this dependence explicit.

11. **OK — Proposition 10, sharpness and the equality subspace (lines 1021–1133).** Normalized alternation is an isometry by orthogonality of distinct orderings. On the product of wedges, a basis weight uses at most $\min(k,h_c)$ of the first $k$ basis vectors in column $c$. This gives the prefix inequalities, including equality at $n$. Reversing basis order gives $\lambda_j=m_{n-j}$. Independently, evaluating the trace-form Casimir on a highest vector gives $\sum_j\mu_j^2+\sum_{i<j}(\mu_i-\mu_j)=\Gamma_n(\mu)$ by the matrix-unit commutators. For the two dominant weights, Abel summation gives $\Gamma_n(\lambda)-\Gamma_n(\mu)=\sum_{k<n}D_k[(\lambda_k-\lambda_{k+1})+(\mu_k-\mu_{k+1})+2]$. Every bracket is positive and $D_n=0$. The equality space among admissible tensors is the Cartan summand on selected slots tensored with the allowed alternating space on unselected slots, equivalently the intersection described at lines 1116–1125.
